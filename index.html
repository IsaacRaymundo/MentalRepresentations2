Research Purpose

The purpose of this research study is to understand how people distribute resources.

What information is on this form?

We are asking you to take part in a research study. This form explains why we are doing this study and what you will be asked to do if you choose to be in this study. It also describes the way we (Researchers) would like to use and share information about you.

Please take the time to read this form. You do not have to participate if you do not want to or feel uncomfortable at any moment.

Why is this study being done?

We are doing this research study to better understand how people enact equality via distributing resources. If you agree to take part in this research, you will be asked to complete a short task and respond to a confidential questionnaire about your beliefs and attitudes about the task and society. Your participation in this study will last approximately 8 minutes. The session today will take place entirely online. You will receive $1.60 for your time and participation.

Do I have to be in the study?

Voluntary Participation. Participation in this study is entirely voluntary. Your decision about whether to participate will have no impact on your employment, student status, or any other entitlements. You may discontinue participation at any time without penalty. If you do choose to participate, the answers given will have no impact on your student status, salary, grade, or employment with Columbia University.

Risks and Benefits

Are there any risks? We do not think that there are any risks to taking part in this study. You can choose to skip questions if they make you uncomfortable.

Are there any benefits? You will not benefit from taking part in this study. However, it is hoped that the research will benefit others (or science) who wish to understand behavioral science and psychology studies.

What about Confidentiality

No information collected during this study will be able to identify you by name. We will do everything we can to keep your data secure, however, complete confidentiality cannot be promised. Despite all of our efforts, unanticipated problems, such as a stolen computer may occur, although it is highly unlikely.

All of your questionnaire responses will be assigned a code number, and separated from any other information that could identify you. The research file that links your participant ID to the code number will be kept in a password-protected computer and only the investigator and study staff will have access to the file.

The following individuals and/or agencies will be able to look at and copy your research records:
 • The investigator(s), members of the research team who may be involved with the study
 • Authorities from Columbia University, including the Institutional Review Board ('IRB')

Background Information

Welcome to Velmara

In Velmara, Xyrenite stones are a valuable resource in the Velmaran society.

Within Velmara live two groups: the Drevians and the Orvans. Both groups rely on Xyrenite stones in their daily lives.

Your task today will involve interacting with these stones and making some decisions about them.

On the next page, you will enter a practice area where you can get familiar with moving Xyrenite stones before the real task begins. Please read the instructions and make sure you understand the task basics before beginning the task.
